### Expense Categories & Specific Rules

 

1. **Transport** (Fuel, Vehicle Registration, WOF, Parking, Public Transit)

2. **Utilities**

  * *Rule:* Calculate a **monthly average** for each utility type based on its nature and billing frequency (e.g., Power, Water, Gas, Internet/Wi-Fi, Phone/Mobile). Show explicit calculation steps for assessor understanding, then sum these calculations into a single **Utility Expenses** total.

3. **Insurance (Unified Master Heading)**

  * *Rule:* Group ALL insurance expenses under this single section, then bifurcate/itemize by policy type:

    * Dining out, cafes, takeaways, and bars

    * Domestic travel & local trips (strictly excluding major or international trips)

6. **Monthly Subscriptions**

  * *Rule:* Separate all recurring digital and non-digital subscriptions into this standalone category. Examples: Gym memberships, gaming services, streaming/OTT platforms (Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, Amazon, Patreon, Google Storage, Apple services).

  * *Required Breakdown:* Perform and display explicit calculations based on payment frequency (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, annual) for full assessor understanding.

7. **Education** (Public & Private tuition, school fees, uniforms)

8. **KiwiSaver & Savings / Investments**

9. **Childcare & Child Support**

10. **Donations / Tithings**

11. **Rent / Board Paid**

12. **Medical Expenses** (Doctors, pharmacy, dental)

13. **Extracurricular Activities** (Includes all sports activities, clubs, lessons)

14. **Other Expenses** (Any recurring expense that cannot reasonably fit within the categories above)

15. **One-Off Expenses** (All non-recurring, unusual, or extraordinary items, including major/international travel)

 

*(Note: Council Rates are intentionally excluded from automated calculations and will be assessed manually by the underwriter.)*

### Strict Accounting Rules

1. **No Grouping as "Various":** Every single transaction must be itemized individually with its exact date and amount. Do not group multiple grocery, fuel, dining, or retail transactions into aggregate entries.

2. **Exclusions from Living Expenses:** Strictly EXCLUDE the following from regular monthly living expenses:

  * Internal transfers between applicant accounts

  * Credit card repayments

  * Loan repayments & Mortgage repayments

  * Interest charges

  * Redraws

  * Income credits & Reimbursements

3. **Payment Frequency Identification:** Determine frequency pattern for recurring items (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual). Convert to Monthly Equivalent using standard formulas:

  * **Weekly:** $Amount \times 52 / 12$

  * **Fortnightly:** $Amount \times 26 / 12$

  * **Quarterly:** $Amount / 3$

  * **Annual:** $Amount / 12$

4. **One-Off Items:** Place all extraordinary or non-recurring items in the **One-Off Expenses** section with a clear reason for exclusion. Do not include these in regular monthly living expense calculations.
